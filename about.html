ABOUT LUMIÈRE

BEAUTY IS IN
THE DETAILS.

Lumière Lash & Brow was created around a simple idea: beauty should feel considered, personal and completely your own.

Every appointment begins with understanding your features, your preferences and the feeling you want to leave with. We approach lash mapping and brow shaping not as a standard service, but as an intimate craft that honors your unique facial architecture.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Beauty, but
make it yours.

At Lumière, there is no single definition of beautiful. The goal is not to create a look that belongs to everyone. It is to create a result that works beautifully with you.

PERSONAL

02

PRECISE

Every placement, curve and shape is considered carefully.

03
