LONGEVITY & CARE

The Essential Aftercare Ritual

Flawless lash extensions and brow lamination are a partnership between salon precision and daily home ritual. Follow our 4 core golden rules to extend retention up to 4+ weeks.

01

Clean Daily with Oil-Free Foam

Use our gentle botanical lash shampoo to dissolve sebum and makeup oils that break down cyanoacrylate adhesive bonds.

02

Keep Completely Dry for First 24H

Avoid steam rooms, intense workouts, and heavy moisture during initial adhesive curing to ensure unbreakable attachment.

03

Brush & Fluff When Dry

Use your complimentary rose-gold silicone spoolie brush only when lashes are fully dry to realign fans without snagging.

04

Schedule 2–3 Week Maintenance Infills

Natural lashes shed 3–5 hairs per day. 60-minute infills replace shed extensions and maintain full, even volume.

Complimentary Aftercare Kit Every new client receives a luxury spoolie brush and custom lash care brochure.
