STUDIO GOVERNANCE & TRUST

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026. LUMIÈRE Lash & Brow Studio is committed to respecting your personal privacy and protecting all appointment details you entrust with us.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an appointment inquiry or contact our studio, we collect your name, email address, phone number, preferred service, date and time preferences, and any specific aesthetic notes you share with our artists.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your contact information solely to coordinate and confirm your beauty appointments, provide customized lash/brow consultations, send appointment reminders, and deliver aftercare guidance tailored to your session.

3. Client Confidentiality & Data Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties. All client details are maintained strictly for studio booking and client care purposes.

4. Inquiries & Data Updates

If you wish to update your contact information or request removal of your booking inquiry records, please reach out directly through our Contact Page.
